Methodology. Generation data: OpenElectricity API at 1-hour intervals (max 32 days per request, fetched as 12 monthly chunks per facility, cached 7 days per facility-month in Redis). Spot prices: AEMO Price & Demand CSVs at 30-min resolution, averaged in pairs to hourly for alignment. Gas dispatch is economically rational — gas fires only when spot price ≥ gas SRMC; otherwise shortfall is purchased from spot directly. P&L uses: flatRevenue = targetMW × flatPrice; netMargin = flatRevenue + curtailmentRevenue − gasCost − spotCost − renewableCost. Coverage = % of hours where (renewable output + gas fired) ≥ target MW. The coverage curve sweeps gas capacity from 0 → target MW in 50 steps using the same renewable output time series. Limitations: 1-hour generation data smooths sub-hourly dispatch events. Curtailment revenue assumes surplus can be exported at spot (negative price hours are excluded). MLF applied to generation output only.
