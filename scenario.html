Methodology & Important Caveats

—

Revenue calculation: Monthly spot revenue = Capacity (MW) × Monthly Capacity Factor × Hours in Month × Fleet GWAP ($/MWh) × MLF. GWAP is sourced from OpenElectricity's monthly fleet-level data for the actual regional wind or solar generation fleet.

Capacity factor: Regional annual averages are sourced from AEMO Generation Information 2024 and AER State of the Energy Market 2024. Seasonal adjustments reflect known wind/solar resource patterns. CF can be overridden with a project-specific value.

This model does not include: FCAS revenue (typically 20–50% of total battery revenue, but less significant for wind/solar), network service payments, LGC/REGO revenue, connection costs, O&M costs, or project-specific curtailment. It is an energy-market revenue simulation only.

Not financial advice. This tool is for indicative commercial analysis. All outputs should be verified against project-specific data and professional advice before use in investment decisions.
