About Spot Prices

Spot prices represent the wholesale price of electricity in the National Electricity Market (NEM) at each 5-minute trading interval, later averaged into 30-minute settlement periods. These prices reflect the real-time balance between electricity supply and demand, fluctuating based on factors including weather conditions, generation availability, fuel costs, and transmission constraints. The charts below show monthly average spot prices over the past 4 years, revealing long-term market trends, seasonal patterns, and the impact of major energy market events.
