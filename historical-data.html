Monthly average and maximum spot prices from AEMO settlement data

About This Data
This page displays real historical electricity price data sourced directly from AEMO (Australian Energy Market Operator). The data comes from actual 5-minute settlement prices across the National Electricity Market (NEM). Each month's data shows the average price calculated from all 5-minute intervals, the maximum price reached during that period, and a breakdown of price events including negative pricing, high pricing ($300-$1,000/MWh), and extreme pricing (≥$1,000/MWh) occurrences.
