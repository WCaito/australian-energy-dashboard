⚠ Revenue shown is energy market only — FCAS payments are not included
NSW batteries earn revenue from two sources: energy market arbitrage (charging cheap, discharging expensive — visible on these charts as power × spot price) and FCAS (Frequency Control Ancillary Services — a capacity payment for being available to respond to frequency events, regardless of whether the battery physically dispatches). The OpenElectricity API exposes only energy market revenue; FCAS clearing prices and enablement data are not available. In periods of high FCAS demand, FCAS can represent 40–60% or more of a battery's total wholesale revenue — explaining why the cumulative revenue line may step upward even when the power chart appears flat.
